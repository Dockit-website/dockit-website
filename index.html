Why it matters

It's bigger than receipts.

That little slip of paper has a bigger footprint than it looks. Our goal is a world where you never need a paper receipt again.

10.6 billion paper receipts are printed in Australia every year. That's about 150,000 trees and 1.5 billion litres of water. UTS research

Better for your health

Most receipts are printed on thermal paper coated with BPA or BPS. These chemicals can rub off onto your skin, and "BPA-free" paper often just swaps in BPS.

Read more: Minnesota Pollution Control Agency · Skin absorption study (NIH) · 2025 receipt study (RSC)

Better for the planet

Thermal receipts can't be recycled in Australia. The coating contaminates recycling, so every one ends up in landfill.

Read more: UTS: The life journey of an average receipt · ACT Government · Shoalhaven City Council · Better Homes and Gardens

Better for the people serving you

Cashiers, baristas and bar staff handle receipts all day, so they're the most exposed. Less paper means less handling.

Read more: Minnesota Pollution Control Agency · Workers Health & Safety Centre
